Join Cub Scout Pack 776

Boys and Girls grades K-5 are welcome to come and participate in various Pack activities, including Fall and Spring campouts, Pinewood Derby, Blue/Gold celebration, religious emblems, outdoor activities, service projects, and more. Meetings are most Sundays 4:00-5:00 PM with a monthly Pack meeting and multiple Den meetings from August-May. Pack 776 hosts several summer opportunities. If you "Request More Information," you will receive an email with more specific information.
