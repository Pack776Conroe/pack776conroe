What to Expect

When you join Pack 776, we want you and your Scout to feel welcome and prepared. Here’s a quick overview of how our program works.

Pack Meetings

Pack 776 meets most Sundays throughout the Conroe ISD school year, all dens come together for a Pack Meeting. Families are encouraged to attend. During our meetings you can expect:

  • Opening and closing flag ceremonies
  • Reciting the Scout Oath and Law and shouting our Scout Motto, "DO YOUR BEST!"/li>
  • Awards and recognition for every Scout rank
  • Fun activities and songs led by Den and Pack Leaders
  • Information about upcoming events
  • A chance for the whole family to share in the Scouting spirit

Scouts are expected to wear their official Field Uniform (Class A) to all Pack Meetings, unless otherwise instructed to wear their pack shirt.

Den Meetings

Dens meet on Sundays from 4:00–5:00 PM and focus on age-appropriate adventures. Den meetings include:

  • Hands-on activities and skill building
  • Games, crafts, and outdoor fun
  • Working toward rank requirements and adventure loops/pins
  • Teamwork and leadership opportunities

Younger dens (Lion and Tiger) typically require a parent or adult partner to participate with the Scout during our meetings.

Campouts & Special Events

Pack 776 offers exciting outdoor and special experiences throughout the year, including:

  • Fall and Spring campouts
  • Special overnight adventures (examples of what we've done include camping overnight at the Houston Zoo, NASA, baseball games, the USS Lexington and more)
  • Pinewood Derby and Raingutter Regatta
  • Blue & Gold celebration
  • Community service projects, participating in the Conroe Christmas Parade and 4th of July Parade
  • Summer opportunities

An adult family member is required to accompany Cub Scouts on overnight campouts.

Cub Scout Ranks

Cub Scouts progress through ranks based on grade level. Each rank focuses on fun adventures that build character, citizenship, fitness, and leadership.

LION

Lion

Kindergarten
Introduction to Scouting with simple, fun adventures done with an adult partner.

TIGER

Tiger

1st Grade
Adventures focused on exploration, teamwork, and trying new things with a parent or adult partner.

WOLF

Wolf

2nd Grade
Outdoor skills, community awareness, and growing independence through required and elective adventures.

BEAR

Bear

3rd Grade
More challenging adventures that build responsibility, outdoor knowledge, and leadership.

WEBELOS

Webelos

4th Grade
Preparation for Scouts BSA with more advanced outdoor skills and leadership opportunities.

AOL

Arrow of Light

5th Grade
The highest rank in Cub Scouting. Completing this prepares Scouts to bridge into a Scouts BSA Troop.

Uniforms

Scouts wear the official Cub Scout uniform (blue for younger ranks, tan option for Webelos/Arrow of Light). The uniform helps build a sense of belonging and is worn for Pack Meetings, special events, and when representing the Pack.

Family Involvement

Cub Scouting is a family program. Parents and guardians are encouraged to participate in meetings, campouts, and service projects. Your involvement makes the experience richer for your Scout and helps the Pack thrive. We welcome everyone's help no matter skills or experience! This is a team effort!
